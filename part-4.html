Prompt Engineering

It's not just typing into a chatbox. It is programming with natural language to strictly constrain the model's output.

The Messy Input Data (Unstructured Text)
"Hey class, just a quick update on the final project. The proposal is due next friday, which I think is Nov 12th. Wait, no, it's Nov 15th. Then we'll do the peer reviews on Nov 22. Make sure the final submission is uploaded by midnight on December 5th!"

The Consumer Prompt

"Extract the deadlines from the text above and give it to me."
Model Output (Unpredictable)

Sure! Here are the deadlines I found in your text:

  • The proposal is due on Friday, Nov 15th.
  • Peer reviews happen on Nov 22.
  • Final submission is Dec 5th at midnight.

Hope this helps! Let me know if you need anything else! Have a great day!

Problem: Try parsing this response with code. It's impossible. It has conversational filler and unpredictable formatting.

The System Instruction

API Mode 
"system_instruction": """
You are an invisible data extraction pipeline.
Task: Extract tasks and dates.
Constraint 1: Output ONLY valid JSON.
Constraint 2: Do NOT include markdown formatting.
Constraint 3: Do NOT say "Here is your JSON".
Format: [{"task": "string", "date": "YYYY-MM-DD"}]
"""
